A concept for Protege’s healthcare focus

Two beacons Protege could own

A beacon is something you publish that keeps drawing a particular audience to your corner of the internet. Scale’s leaderboards draw people who follow benchmarks. Ramp’s AI Index draws people tracking who’s actually buying AI. On a recent podcast, Protege cofounder Engy Ziedan offered an interesting way to think about the data business: data for a solve, not data for a sale. That idea applies across both sides of Protege’s marketplace. Here are two beacons that could bring more of the right people into that conversation.

01Beacon 01

Reality Gap

Model makers should not grade their own homework. Protege has already made that case, and in October it scored four frontier models on Disease Odyssey. This turns that work into a live page, updated every quarter: the newest public models run on real, de-identified records, and the page shows where each model holds up, and where scores fall, when it has to name the diagnosis instead of picking it from a list.

Why it earns attention

A health system deciding whether to license its data wants to know which problems its data could help solve. Protege sees those problems across many builders at once, which gives health systems a reason to call Protege.

It also gives press and investors a recurring signal to quote, which keeps Protege in the healthcare AI conversation between launches.

Open questions. Aggregate data only, never anything that identifies a customer or dataset. What builders are working on may be part of Protege’s edge, so the index should publish broad problem areas and direction, not exact shares, and only with enough builders in each category that no single one can be identified. Protege already names frontier models on Disease Odyssey, but publishing a result a builder commissions stays the builder’s choice. Repeat runs need fresh held-out cases so the test stays uncontaminated. Protege-prepared data also powers Vals AI’s MedScribe and MedCode benchmarks, so this should complement that work, not compete with it. Beacon 01 extends the evaluation service and Disease Odyssey rather than replacing them. It turns one-off results into a recurring reason for builders to come in.
